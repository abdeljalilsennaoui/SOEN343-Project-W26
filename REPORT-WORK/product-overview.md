# Product Overview

> **Smart Urban Mobility Management System (SUMMS)**  
> A unified web platform built to make urban transportation more accessible through one central system.

---

SUMMS was designed as a single platform that brings together different urban mobility services in one place. Instead of requiring users to move between multiple separate applications, the system gives them access to transportation-related services through one unified interface.

The platform is designed with a user-centered approach and supports different types of users through role-based access. This allows SUMMS to respond to different needs while keeping the overall system organized and easier to use.

Overall, the objective of the platform is to make transportation through the city more efficient, connected, and simple to access.
